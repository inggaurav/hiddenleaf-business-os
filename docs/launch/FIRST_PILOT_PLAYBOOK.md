# First Pilot Customer Playbook

## 1. Pilot Customer Criteria
* Target Size: 5–50 team members.
* Core Needs: Invoicing, CRM, Project Management, and Unified Customer Messaging.
* Engagement Duration: 30-day guided pilot.

---

## 2. Onboarding & Deployment Sequence

### Day 1: Provisioning & Discovery
1. Self-register primary account on `https://app.hiddenleaf.agency/register`.
2. Complete 6-step Onboarding Wizard (Company name, currency, timezone, Brand Profile).
3. Super Admin extends trial to 30-day Pilot period.
4. Invite core stakeholders (Admin, Finance Lead, Sales Lead).

### Day 2–3: Integration & Knowledge Ingestion
1. Connect primary corporate communications (Gmail Google Workspace OAuth or WhatsApp Cloud API).
2. Upload company onboarding docs, service catalog, and standard proposals to Knowledge Center.
3. Verify test connection status in Settings $\to$ Integrations.

### Week 1–2: Active Operations
1. Import initial customer and lead records via standard CSV import.
2. Team begins daily operations in CRM and Taskly.
3. Review daily Executive Briefings in Command Center.

### Week 3–4: Automation & Autonomous Missions
1. Activate 2–3 standard deterministic automations (e.g. New Lead Notification, Overdue Invoice Alert).
2. Launch first Mr. Fox Mission (e.g. Inactive Account Health Check).
3. Review Unified Approval Center log.

### Day 30: Commercial Transition
1. Review ROI summary (messages handled, invoices tracked, hours saved).
2. Transition to paid SaaS subscription (Growth or Business tier).
