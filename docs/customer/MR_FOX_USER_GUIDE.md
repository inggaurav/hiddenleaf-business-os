# Mr. Fox Executive AI — User Guide

## 1. What is Mr. Fox?
**Mr. Fox** is the built-in executive intelligence agent for HiddenLeaf Business OS. Unlike generic chatbots, Mr. Fox is deeply connected to your ERP ledger, sales pipeline, communications, and knowledge base.

---

## 2. Core Capabilities

### A. Real-Time Operational Inquiries
Ask Mr. Fox natural language questions about your business:
* *"What is our cash and receivables position today?"*
* *"Which invoices are currently overdue?"*
* *"Show me high-priority tasks due this week."*
* *"Do we have any low-stock inventory items that need reordering?"*

Mr. Fox executes deterministic backend tools to fetch exact database figures and provides clickable evidence pills linking directly to relevant records.

### B. Brand-Aligned Communication Replies
In the **Unified Inbox** (`/inbox`), Mr. Fox can draft client replies based on the conversation history and your organization's **Brand Profile**. You review and edit the draft before sending.

### C. Governed Document Knowledge & RAG
Upload contracts, pricing sheets, or internal SOPs to the **Knowledge Center** (`/knowledge`). You can then ask:
* *"What is our cancellation policy according to the master service agreement?"*
Mr. Fox will answer with verifiable document citations.

---

## 3. Human-in-the-Loop Safety & Approvals
Mr. Fox is built with strict governance. Any high-risk action—such as sending an external email, modifying an invoice, or issuing a refund—is routed to the **Unified Approval Center** (`/command-center/approvals`) for human authorization before execution.
