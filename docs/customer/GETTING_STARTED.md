# HiddenLeaf Business OS — Getting Started Guide

Welcome to **HiddenLeaf Business OS**, the comprehensive enterprise operating system powered by **Mr. Fox Executive AI**.

---

## 1. Quick Setup in 3 Minutes

1. **Sign Up**: Create your organization account at `/register`.
2. **Complete the Wizard**:
   * Set your company name, operating currency (e.g. USD, EUR, INR), and timezone.
   * Enter a brief Brand Description so Mr. Fox understands your business tone.
   * Invite your primary team members (e.g., Finance Lead, Sales Lead).
3. **Explore Demo Data**: You can immediately load realistic sample data to see the Executive Command Center in action.

---

## 2. Navigating the Core Modules

* **Executive Command Center** (`/command-center`): View your deterministic 0–100 Business Health score, ranked priorities, and morning briefing.
* **Unified Communications** (`/inbox`): Manage client communications across Gmail, WhatsApp, Slack, and internal messaging.
* **Sales & CRM** (`/crm/leads`, `/sales/invoices`): Track pipeline deals and issue professional, itemized invoices.
* **Taskly Project Management** (`/taskly/projects`): Organize deliverables into milestones and kanban task boards.
* **Governed Knowledge** (`/knowledge`): Upload company standard operating procedures (SOPs), manuals, and contracts for citation-backed AI questions.
* **Autonomous Missions & Automations** (`/missions`, `/automations`): Automate repetitive tasks and launch goal-driven AI missions.

---

## 3. Keyboard Shortcut
Press **`Cmd + K`** (or **`Ctrl + K`** on Windows) anywhere in the application to open the **Global Command Palette** and instantly search across leads, invoices, messages, tasks, and knowledge documents.
