# HiddenLeaf Business OS — 12-Minute Sales Demonstration Script

## 1. Executive Summary & Flow
* **Audience**: Business Owners, Managing Directors, Operations VPs, Agency Founders.
* **Duration**: 10–12 minutes.
* **Goal**: Demonstrate how HiddenLeaf Business OS acts as an autonomous operating system with Mr. Fox providing real-time operational intelligence, unified customer communications, and human-in-the-loop task execution.

---

## 2. Demonstration Flow

### Minute 0–2: The Executive Command Center
* **Action**: Open `/command-center`.
* **Talking Points**:
  * "Traditional ERPs are static filing cabinets. HiddenLeaf is an active command center."
  * Highlight the **Business Health Score (e.g. 95/100)**: "Calculated deterministically from actual ledger and operational records—not vague LLM guesswork."
  * Show **Ranked Executive Priorities**: Point to overdue receivables or urgent customer escalations with estimated financial impact.

### Minute 2–4: Ask Mr. Fox (Cross-Domain Intelligence)
* **Action**: Open Mr. Fox Panel (`Cmd+K` or sidebar widget).
* **Prompt**: *"What is our current receivables position, and which invoices are overdue?"*
* **Talking Points**:
  * Observe Mr. Fox querying the `executive.health` and `sales.outstanding` tools in real time.
  * Point out the **Evidence Pills** linking directly to the underlying invoice records with zero hallucinated URLs.

### Minute 4–6: Unified Communications Inbox
* **Action**: Navigate to `/inbox`.
* **Talking Points**:
  * "All client messages across Gmail, WhatsApp, and Slack are unified in one inbox."
  * Open an urgent client thread. Show the AI Attention Score (88/100) and Sentiment indicator.
  * Click **"Draft Brand-Aligned Reply with Mr. Fox"**: Note how Mr. Fox references company Brand Profile guidelines to write a contextual reply.

### Minute 6–8: Governed Knowledge & RAG
* **Action**: Navigate to `/knowledge`.
* **Talking Points**:
  * Show company policy or standard operating procedure document.
  * Ask Mr. Fox a specific procedural question.
  * Highlight the exact **Document Citation** and verification snippet.

### Minute 8–10: Automations & Mr. Fox Missions
* **Action**: Navigate to `/missions` and `/automations`.
* **Talking Points**:
  * "Deterministic automation rules handle routine business workflows with zero token cost."
  * "Intelligent Mr. Fox Missions handle complex multi-step objectives (e.g., Q3 Inactive Lead Re-engagement)."
  * Show the **Unified Approval Center** (`/command-center/approvals`): "High-risk actions require explicit human sign-off before sending or modifying live data."

### Minute 10–12: Instant Onboarding & Next Steps
* **Action**: Show Settings & Onboarding Wizard.
* **Call to Action**: "You can self-provision your workspace today with a 14-day full-featured trial."
